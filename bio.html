Bio

A long-running curiosity about people, things, and systems (how affordances emerge, how trends travel, how making shapes thinking) is what drew me into design.

Over the past five years I've worked across finance, social apps, and legal technology, consistently drawn to where design intersects with trust, safety, and governance.

I use generative systems and AI as design tools to model behaviour, surface patterns, and identify design and product opportunities.

Approach

☼

I bridge interaction design methods and systems thinking to create meaningful, evolving designs. Drawing on industry and HCI research experience, I translate complex insights into practical applications.

My personal interests include biological self-organization, morphological computation, and distributed cognition - I am investigating how these principles might eventually inform more adaptive and responsive design approaches. I'm always looking for collaboration opportunities with researchers and practitioners with similar interests.

Currently expanding my expertise in AI fairness auditing and algorithmic governance to support responsible AI deployment
