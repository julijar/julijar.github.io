Bio

A long-running curiosity about people, things, and systems (how affordances emerge, how trends travel, how making shapes thinking) is what drew me into design.

Over the past six years I've worked across financial, social apps, legal technology, and digital governance — consistently drawn to where design intersects with accountability, trust, and democratic participation.

I have published research in community governance systems and AI-mediated design, and apply generative AI as a design tool to model behaviour, surface patterns, and identify product opportunities.

Interests

Systemic design, semiotics, biological self-organization, morphological computation, and distributed cognition — and how these might inform more adaptive, responsive design. Always open to collaborating with researchers and practitioners with similar interests.
